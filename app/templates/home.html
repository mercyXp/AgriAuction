AgriAuction Digital Produce Trading Platform

Digital Agricultural Produce Trading Made Simple

AgriAuction is a digital platform for managing agricultural produce auctions. It connects farmers with registered buyers and helps authorised staff manage bidding, sales, payments and collections from one system.

Get Started

About

A clearer way to run produce auctions

What AgriAuction is

AgriAuction is a web-based produce trading system. Farmers register lots at depots, buyers place bids, and staff record the sale, payment and collection that follow.

The problem it addresses

Paper-based auction records are easy to lose, hard to check, and difficult to report on. Duplicate sales, missed payments and unclear winners become more likely.

How it digitizes auctions

Each lot, bid, sale, payment and collection is stored as related records. Auction closing follows a controlled process so the highest valid bid can become the sale.

Why a relational database

A centralized MySQL database keeps farmers, buyers, lots, bids, sales, payments and collections consistent through keys, constraints and transactions.

Who it is built for

Built for the people in the auction chain

Farmers

Farmers can register and manage agricultural produce lots that are offered for auction.

Buyers

Registered buyers can view eligible auction lots and submit valid bids.

Auction Staff

Authorized staff can manage farmers, buyers, produce lots, auctions and successful sales.

Finance & Operations

Authorized staff can record payments, monitor outstanding balances and manage produce collections.

Features

What the management system will support

Produce Lot Management

Inspected lots are stored with farmer, grade, depot, quantity and auction window.

Auction & Bidding

Only active buyers can bid on open lots that still meet the minimum price.

Sales Management

A successful sale is created from the winning bid so a lot cannot be sold twice.

Payment Tracking

Verified payments are recorded against a sale without exceeding the sale amount.

Collection Management

Buyers collect purchased produce after the required payment condition is met.

Reports & Summaries

Staff can review supply, purchases, sales and outstanding balances from SQL queries.

Role-Based Access

Application roles such as Admin, Operator, Auction Clerk, Finance and Viewer control what staff can do.

Relational Database Management

MySQL stores the records, relationships and business rules that the web application uses.
