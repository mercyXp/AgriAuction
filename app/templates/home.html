Zambian produce auctions

AgriAuction Digital Produce Trading Platform

Farmers register produce lots at depots. Registered buyers place bids. When an auction closes, the highest valid bid becomes a successful sale.

Register produce

Active farmers send inspected lots to depots with a grade, quantity, and minimum bid.

Run auctions

Buyers bid on open lots. Closing an auction picks the highest valid bid, or marks the lot unsold.

Settle sales

Payments are recorded against a sale. Collection is allowed only when payment rules are met.
