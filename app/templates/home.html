About AgriAuction

A Smarter Way to Manage Agricultural Auctions

What is AgriAuction?

AgriAuction is a web-based system that helps agricultural organizations and market operators efficiently manage produce auctions from listing to collection.

The Problem

Manual auction processes lead to delays, errors, lack of transparency and poor record keeping. Farmers and buyers need a fair, transparent and efficient platform.

Our Solution

AgriAuction digitizes the entire auction process — from produce registration and bidding to sales, payments and collections — ensuring accuracy, transparency and efficiency.

Why a Centralized Database?

A centralized relational database ensures data integrity, consistency and real-time access to accurate information across all operations including farms, buyers, lots, bids, sales, payments and collections.

Who it is built for

Who AgriAuction is built for

Farmers

Farmers can register and manage agricultural produce lots that are offered for auction.

Buyers

Registered buyers can view eligible auction lots and submit valid bids.

Auction Staff

Authorized staff can manage farmers, buyers, produce lots, auctions and successful sales.

Finance & Operations

Authorized staff can record payments, monitor outstanding balances and manage produce collections.

Key Features

Key features

Produce Lot Management

Register and manage different types of agricultural produce lots with quality grades.

Auction & Bidding

Open auctions for lots and enable registered buyers to place valid bids.

Sales Management

Record successful sales and maintain complete sale transaction history.

Payment Tracking

Record payments, track outstanding balances and view payment history.

Collection Management

Schedule and record produce collections by buyers after successful payments.
